HDT‑333 — VTOL Cargo UAV
Diploma thesis · Concept, design & prototype · 2026
Grade 10/10
  • VTOL tandem-wing layout — length ~3 m, carbon-fibre (Twill 3K), 1.92 m² wing area.
  • Hybrid propulsion: 23 kW gasoline generator + 24S HV Li-ion (≈14.3 kWh). Endurance ~67 min.
  • Nine-motor layout — 8 lift + 1 cruise, up to 50 kg payload, ~0.42 m³ cargo, MTOW 240 kg.
  • Aerodynamic analysis via COMSOL Multiphysics — lift, drag, pressure distribution.
  • Techno-economic evaluation — cost of development, market applications, financial projections.
  • 1:6 scale flying prototype — validated flight behaviour and subsystem integration.
  • Pixhawk-6C flight controller with autonomous navigation and safety flight modes.
  • Dual parachute system for emergency recovery — high climb rate design.
  • Structural design in carbon-fibre with manufacturing-oriented approach.
