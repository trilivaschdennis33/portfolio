HDT‑333 — VTOL Cargo UAV Thesis · Grade 10/10

Diploma thesis • Concept, design & prototype · 2026

The HDT‑333 is a VTOL cargo UAV designed for high‑efficiency parcel delivery and same‑day logistics. The project covered system architecture, hybrid propulsion design, full‑scale performance estimation and the development of a 1:6‑scale prototype to validate flight behaviour.

Combined aerodynamic analysis (COMSOL Multiphysics), carbon‑fibre structural design and techno‑economic evaluation.

  • VTOL tandem‑wing layout — overall length ~3 m, carbon‑fibre (Twill 3K) primary structure, 1.92 m² wing area.
  • Hybrid propulsion: 23 kW gasoline generator + 24S HV Li‑ion (≈14.3 kWh total).
  • Nine‑motor layout — up to 50 kg payload, ~0.42 m³ cargo, MTOW 240 kg, cruise ~100 km/h, ~67 min endurance.
  • Pixhawk‑6C flight controller, dual parachute safety system.
  • 1:6 scale flying prototype built and tested to validate flight behaviour and subsystem integration.

Infinity Touch — Touching the Stratosphere Mission Complete

Personal R&D project • 2021

Experimental high‑altitude balloon project aimed at reaching the lower stratosphere with a custom-built capsule. Over several iterations the internal layout, payload integration and reliability were progressively refined.

The final capsule, "HAEPHAESTUS II", carried multiple cameras, communication devices and a custom aerodynamic parachute system.

  • Designed and built five capsule versions with improving internal space management and equipment mounting.
  • Integrated action cameras, security camera, power bank and SIM-connected phone for live tracking.
  • Obtained official flight permission from the Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA).
  • 17/10/2021 — mission completed: ~2.5-hour flight reaching ~30 km altitude.
  • Capsule crossed the Corinthian Gulf and landed safely at sea near Antikyra — concept fully validated.
